# Every Feed Item is a paid Ad, and the app says so

Stafeed's identity is an openly ironic, ads-only feed: unlike other apps it doesn't hide ads among content; the ads are the content, and every item is labelled AD. To keep that claim literally true, the feed only shows items that earn money (Brand Ads paid directly, affiliate or pay-per-click products). Products that pay nothing, such as most Shopify Global Catalog items, are excluded, even though that means a smaller catalog at launch. This deviates from the product spec's "shopping-first, include any relevant product" stance and from its plan to defer brand-supplied creatives (and video) to a later phase: Brand Ads and video playback are in the first build. Meta/Google ad libraries are still never used as a source; Brand Ads come directly from brands with permission. The tone stays light: fun copy around the ads, but it remains a shopping app, and ads are shown unedited.
