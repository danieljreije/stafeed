# For You is all paid Ads, and the app says so

Stafeed's identity is an openly ironic, ads-only feed: unlike other apps it doesn't hide ads among content; the ads are the content, and every Ad is labelled AD. To keep that claim literally true, For You only shows items that earn money (Brand Ads paid directly, affiliate or pay-per-click products). The single exception: inside a Shopping Session, when paid Ads run thin for the goal, products that earn nothing (Shopify Global Catalog) may fill the gaps, labelled honestly as Not an Ad in the same light voice; paid Ads still rank first when relevance is about equal. This deviates from the product spec's "include any relevant product" stance and from its plan to defer brand-supplied creatives (and video) to a later phase: Brand Ads and video playback are in the first build. Meta/Google ad libraries are still never used as a source; Brand Ads come directly from brands with permission. The tone stays light: fun copy around the ads, but it remains a shopping app, and ads are shown unedited.
